FiQuo: money skills for teens

FiQuo is a free financial literacy game for teenagers. Learn how to budget your pocket money, save for something big, understand compound interest, avoid debt traps, invest wisely and spot online scams.

What you can do

  • Bite-sized lessons on pocket money, budgeting, saving, banking, taxes, credit, investing and money safety, with quizzes.
  • 10 mini-games including Needs vs Wants, Budget Battle, Scam Simulator, Loan Detective, Inflation Dodge and a Credit Score Challenge.
  • Life Simulator: live a year of teen money life with real choices, random events and "What if?" replays.
  • Portfolio Simulator: invest fictional money through real historical periods and learn why markets rise and fall.
  • Streaks, weekly quests, leaderboards and badges to keep learning fun.

Country modes for India, the US and the UK. No real money, no sign-up, works on phones, tablets and laptops.
